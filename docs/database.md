Images are formed by compositing individuals from an annotated database of single whole fish (examples shown above). 
The database comprises 4,923 individuals of 26 species that have been collected during projects involving [Marine Scotland](https://masts.ac.uk/resource/marine-laboratory/), [CEFAS](https://www.cefas.co.uk/), and [UEA](https://my.uea.ac.uk/), funded by the Scottish Government and the European Union.

The database represents fish species, collected from conveyor belts aboard 7 vessels (see above).
Most individuals (4,281, 87%) come from belt_F; the others from belt_B (367), belt_C (95), belt_A (89), belt_E (55), belt_D (22) and belt_G (14).
The commonest species are whiting (1,388), haddock (1,341), plaice (457), long rough dab (362) and grey gurnard (267).
The inventory is split into train (3,938), val (492) and test (493) individuals.
<div>&nbsp;</div>

The full database is published on Zenodo as "Segmented Whole Fish" ([doi:10.5281/zenodo.23238609](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23238609), CC BY 4.0); access is restricted, so request it there. We also provide a small sample database of 100 individuals, sufficient to run "./demo/demo.py"
